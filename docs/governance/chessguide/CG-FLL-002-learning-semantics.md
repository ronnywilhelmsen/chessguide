## Context

[CG-FLL-001](CG-FLL-001-first-domain-learning-pilot.md) established the first operational learning pilot.

The pilot revealed a deeper question:

**What is learning?**

Before LearningTrace design, runtime architectures, federation validation, or Android pilots can be specified, the **meaning of learning** must be clarified.

This document defines **learning semantics**.

**Out of scope:** Implementation, storage, APIs, runtime architecture, UI.

---

## Purpose

Define:

- What learning is
- What learning is not
- How learning differs from activity
- How learning differs from knowledge
- How learning differs from recall
- How expertise changes observation
- Why integration matters
- Why simulation matters
- How learning transforms future capability

---

## Core Question

**How can learning be recognized?**

---

## Primary Principle

> **Learning is not information received.**
>
> **Learning is integration achieved.**

---

## Learning vs Activity

**Activity may occur without learning.**

Examples:

- Playing chess
- Reading books
- Watching videos
- Solving puzzles

Activity alone is **not** evidence of learning.

**Learning requires transformation.**

| Concept | Definition |
|---------|------------|
| **Activity** | Behaviour or exposure in the domain |
| **Learning** | Integration that changes future capability |

---

## Learning vs Knowledge

**Knowledge is not learning.**

| Concept | Role |
|---------|------|
| **Knowledge** | A resource — stored, referenced, possessed |
| **Learning** | The process that creates, organizes, integrates, and applies knowledge |

A learner may **possess knowledge** without effectively using it.

Learning is demonstrated when knowledge becomes **usable under observation** — not merely retained.

---

## Learning vs Recall

**Recall is not learning.**

| Concept | Role |
|---------|------|
| **Recall** | Retrieval |
| **Learning** | Makes retrieval possible and meaningful |

### Learning Path

```
Attention
  ↓
Encoding
  ↓
Integration
  ↓
Knowledge Formation
```

### Recall Path

```
Attention
  ↓
Retrieval
  ↓
Knowledge Use
```

Recall without prior integration may succeed briefly but does not constitute durable learning.

---

## Reality and Observation

**Reality exists independently.**

**Observation does not.**

Observation is **reality interpreted through existing learning**.

Different actors may share the same reality while observing different things.

| Pair | Same reality | Different observation |
|------|--------------|---------------------|
| Cat and physician | Patient / body | Symptom vs vital sign |
| Beginner and grandmaster | Position on board | Threat vs structure |
| Novice and expert | Practice session | Moves vs patterns |

---

## Observation Capacity

**Learning changes observation.**

The learner increasingly recognizes:

- Patterns
- Structures
- Relationships
- Opportunities
- Risks

— **without changes to reality itself**.

### Hypothesis H1

> **Learning increases observation capacity.**

What was invisible becomes observable as integration deepens.

---

## Expertise

**Expertise is not merely accumulated knowledge.**

Expertise **reduces the distance** between:

```
Observation
  ↓
Knowledge
```

through **integration**.

Integrated learning enables observation to rapidly activate:

```
Attention
  ↓
Memory
  ↓
Knowledge
```

without extensive conscious reasoning.

### Hypothesis H2

> **Integrated learning shortens the distance between observation and knowledge.**

---

## Integration

**Integration is the central learning mechanism.**

### Definition

> **Integration** is the process of connecting new information to existing structures and making it usable.

### Potential integration mechanisms

- Reflection
- Explanation
- Simulation
- Comparison
- Teaching
- Application
- Discussion
- Consolidation

### Hypotheses H4 and H8

| ID | Statement |
|----|-----------|
| **H4** | Integration is required for durable learning |
| **H8** | Exposure enables learning; integration makes learning durable |

---

## Simulation

**Humans learn through simulation.**

Examples:

- Imagined futures
- Candidate moves
- Hypothetical positions
- Thought experiments
- Mental rehearsal

**Simulation is a first-class learning mechanism.**

### Federation insight

| Domain | Simulation mode |
|--------|-----------------|
| **Chess** | Internal simulation (mental board, variations) |
| **Finkairos** | External simulation (scenario, outcome modelling) |

Both explore **possible futures**.

### Hypothesis H3

> **Simulation is a primary learning mechanism.**

---

## Continuity

**Learning is cumulative.**

Continuity may be a stronger predictor of mastery than intensity.

### Hypothesis H6

> **Repeated engagement over time produces greater transformation than isolated periods of intense activity.**

Continuity supports integration across episodes; intensity without continuity may produce activity without durable learning.

---

## Engagement

**Learning is influenced by motivational state.**

### Potential signals

- Curiosity
- Enjoyment
- Intrinsic motivation
- Engagement
- Exploration

### Hypothesis H7

> **Positive engagement increases continuity.**

```
Positive engagement → increased continuity → improved integration → increased learning
```

---

## Mentorship

**Mentors accelerate integration.**

A mentor may compress years of experience into immediately useful guidance.

Examples:

- Socratic dialogue
- Coaching
- Demonstration
- Guided discovery
- Explanation

### Federation role

| Entity | Provides |
|--------|----------|
| **Laris** | Pedagogical guide — learning guidance |
| **ChessGuide** (and other domains) | Domain knowledge |

Laris develops **learning understanding**; domains develop **domain understanding**.

---

## Biological Considerations

**Learning is not purely cognitive.**

### Potential contributors

- Sleep
- Recovery
- Energy
- Stress
- Biological rhythms

### Future federation collaboration

**BioChronos** may provide longitudinal biological context for learning trajectories — informing integration windows and recovery, not replacing domain observation.

---

## Learning Transformation

**Evidence of learning** may include:

- Improved observation
- Faster recognition
- Improved recall
- Improved transfer
- Improved judgment
- Improved stewardship
- Behavioral change

**Learning is ultimately demonstrated through transformed capability.**

### Hypothesis H5

> **Learning is observable through transformation rather than activity.**

---

## Learning Chain Interpretation

```
Reality
  ↓
Observation
  ↓
Attention
  ↓
Understanding
  ↓
Knowledge
  ↓
Wisdom
  ↓
Stewardship
  ↓
Transformation
```

The chain is **dynamic** rather than strictly sequential.

Different levels may activate **recursively**, **simultaneously**, or **rapidly** depending on expertise.

| Learner state | Chain behaviour |
|---------------|-----------------|
| Novice | More sequential; explicit Attention and Understanding |
| Expert | Compressed paths; Observation → Knowledge with less conscious intermediate steps |
| All stages | Stewardship and Transformation remain accountable over time |

Consistent with [CB-000A — Longitudinal Learning Model](../chessbuddy/CB-000A-longitudinal-learning-model.md).

## Semantic Hypotheses (summary)

| ID | Hypothesis |
|----|------------|
| **H1** | Learning increases observation capacity |
| **H2** | Integrated learning shortens the distance between observation and knowledge |
| **H3** | Simulation is a primary learning mechanism |
| **H4** | Integration is required for durable learning |
| **H5** | Learning is observable through transformation rather than activity |
| **H6** | Continuity is a stronger predictor of mastery than intensity |
| **H7** | Positive engagement increases continuity |
| **H8** | Exposure enables learning; integration makes learning durable |

These hypotheses inform [CG-FLL-001](CG-FLL-001-first-domain-learning-pilot.md) observation targets and steward validation.

---

## Recognition Criteria (how learning can be recognized)

Learning may be **recognized** when evidence shows:

| Criterion | Indicator |
|-----------|-----------|
| **Integration** | New information connected to prior structures; usable in new contexts |
| **Transformation** | Changed capability over time — not single-episode performance |
| **Observation shift** | Learner sees patterns previously missed (same reality) |
| **Distinguishable from activity** | Progress persists beyond mere repetition or exposure |
| **Traceable** | Chain lineage from Observation through Stewardship to Transformation claim |

Learning is **not recognized** from activity volume, recall alone, or knowledge possession alone.
