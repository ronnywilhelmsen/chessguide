## Core Question

**What role does ChessGuide play within the Federation?**

---

## Federation Position

ChessGuide is the **first operational domain** focused on longitudinal skill development.

Its purpose is to **observe, understand, and improve skill development over time** through the domain of chess.

ChessGuide is **not** the Federation.

ChessGuide is **one domain** within the Federation.

---

## Relationship to the Continuity-Based Learning Platform

The Continuity-Based Learning Platform seeks to understand learning across domains.

### ChessGuide provides

- A bounded domain
- Observable skill progression
- Replayable learning trajectories
- LearningTrace generation
- Validation opportunities

ChessGuide serves as a **practical learning laboratory**.

---

## Relationship to Laris

| Entity | Role |
|--------|------|
| **Laris** | Federation guide |
| **ChessGuide** | Chess skill domain |

### Responsibilities

**ChessGuide** develops understanding of:

- Chess
- Chess skill
- Chess improvement
- Longitudinal skill development

**Laris** develops understanding of:

- Learning
- Teaching
- Guidance
- Explanation
- Cross-domain learning

### Principle

ChessGuide develops **domain understanding**.

Laris develops **learning understanding**.

Together they transform **experience into learning**.

---

## Relationship to Creator

**Creator** develops:

- Observation
- Attention
- Anchors
- Reasoning
- Continuity
- Knowledge structures

ChessGuide **benefits from** these capabilities.

Creator is **not** a chess domain.

ChessGuide is **not** a continuity platform.

| Entity | Provides |
|--------|----------|
| **Creator** | Foundational learning infrastructure |
| **ChessGuide** | Domain validation |

## Relationship to BioChronos

**BioChronos** develops longitudinal understanding of **biological change**.

**ChessGuide** develops longitudinal understanding of **skill change**.

### Shared concepts

- Trajectories
- Transformation
- Longitudinal analysis
- Progress over time

### Differences

- Biology versus skill

**Relationship:** Mutual learning opportunity.

## Relationship to Future Domains

ChessGuide is expected to **inform future skill domains**.

Examples:

- Music
- Language learning
- Mathematics
- Programming
- Sports

The goal is **not** to generalize chess.

The goal is to **generalize learning**.

---

## Ownership

### ChessGuide owns

- Chess domain understanding
- Skill development understanding
- LearningTrace generation within the chess domain
- Chess-specific observations
- Chess-specific transformations

### ChessGuide does not own

- Federation governance
- General learning guidance
- Cross-domain identity
- Laris responsibilities

---

## Contribution and Consumption (summary)

| Direction | ChessGuide |
|-----------|------------|
| **Contributes** | Bounded domain, LearningTraces, skill trajectories, longitudinal validation |
| **Consumes** | Federation alignment, learning platform concepts, Creator infrastructure (where applicable), Laris guidance |
| **Does not own** | Federation-wide governance, cross-domain learning guidance, Laris role |

---

## Guiding Principle

```
Federation
  ↓
Learning Platform
  ↓
Laris
  ↓
Domain
```

ChessGuide is the **first operational domain**.

Its responsibility is **not** to teach everything.

Its responsibility is to **learn deeply within chess**.

---

## Success Criteria

ChessGuide succeeds within the Federation when:

- It produces high-quality LearningTraces
- It validates longitudinal learning concepts
- It helps explain skill development
- It contributes insights useful beyond chess
- It remains a **focused domain** rather than a general platform
