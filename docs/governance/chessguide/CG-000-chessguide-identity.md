## Identity Question

**What is ChessGuide?**

---

## Core Insight

ChessGuide is **not** primarily a chess application.

ChessGuide is a **longitudinal skill development domain**.

| Dimension | Role |
|-----------|------|
| **Chess** | The domain |
| **Learning** | The mission |

---

## Federation Position

ChessGuide is the **first operational skill-development domain** of the **Continuity-Based Learning Platform**.

Its purpose is to **observe, understand, guide, and improve skill development over time**.

ChessGuide remains aligned with federation concepts established in the ChessBuddy governance lineage — including Federation Alignment, the Longitudinal Learning Model, the Longitudinal Skill Development Domain, and LearningTrace — as **semantic inheritance**, not as repository identity.

## What ChessGuide Is Not

ChessGuide is **not**:

- A Chess.com clone
- A Lichess clone
- A tournament platform
- A social network
- An analysis tool only
- An engine wrapper
