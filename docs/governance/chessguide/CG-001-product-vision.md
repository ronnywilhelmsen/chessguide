## Vision Question

**What does the world look like if ChessGuide succeeds?**

---

## Core Insight

| Traditional chess systems | ChessGuide |
|---------------------------|------------|
| Measure **games** | Measure **learning** |
| Record **outcomes** | Record **development** |
| Answer: *What happened?* | Answer: *How did the player change?* |

---

## Vision

A person should be able to understand their **skill development over time**.

Not only:

- Rating
- Wins
- Losses

But:

- Understanding
- Habits
- Strengths
- Weaknesses
- Improvement patterns
- Transformation

**Chess becomes a visible learning journey.**

---

## Longitudinal Skill Development

ChessGuide seeks to make skill development **observable**.

```
Reality
  ↓
Observation
  ↓
Attention
  ↓
Understanding
  ↓
Knowledge
  ↓
Wisdom
  ↓
Stewardship
  ↓
Transformation
```

The learning chain becomes **visible over time**.

---

## Role of LearningTrace

LearningTrace preserves **learning continuity**.

A player should be able to answer:

- What have I learned?
- How did I learn it?
- Why did I improve?
- Why did I stagnate?
- What should I learn next?

## Individual Value

ChessGuide helps individuals:

- Learn intentionally
- Improve systematically
- Understand themselves
- Understand how they learn
- Build long-term mastery

---

## Federation Value

ChessGuide serves as:

- The first operational skill domain
- A learning laboratory
- A validation environment
- A source of longitudinal learning research

Insights from ChessGuide may inform **future domains**.

---

## Success Criteria

ChessGuide succeeds when:

A player can see not only **games**, but **learning**.

Not only **moves**, but **understanding**.

Not only **performance**, but **transformation**.

---

## What ChessGuide Is Not

ChessGuide is not attempting to become:

- A social network
- A tournament platform
- A game portal
- A chess engine wrapper

Those may exist within the ecosystem. They are **not the mission**.
