## Context

[CG-FLL-001](CG-FLL-001-first-domain-learning-pilot.md) established that learning **may be observed**.

[CG-FLL-002](CG-FLL-002-learning-semantics.md) established learning semantics and proposed:

> **Learning is integration achieved.**

A deeper question remains:

**How does learning become integrated?**

This document defines **Learning Continuity Semantics**.

**Out of scope:** Implementation, storage, APIs, runtime architecture, UI.

---

## Purpose

Define:

- What learning continuity is
- How integration occurs over time
- How continuity differs from exposure
- How continuity differs from activity
- How continuity produces transformation
- How expertise emerges through continuity
- How continuity relates to longitudinal learning

---

## Core Question

**How does learning become integrated over time?**

---

## Primary Principle

> **Learning is integration.**
>
> **Longitudinal learning is integration through continuity.**

---

## Learning Continuity

### Definition

> **Learning Continuity** is the sustained progression through which observations, experiences, knowledge, understanding, and wisdom become increasingly integrated and accessible over time.

Learning continuity is **not** a single event.

Learning continuity is a **developmental process**.

---

## Exposure vs Continuity

| Concept | Role |
|---------|------|
| **Exposure** | Introduces information |
| **Continuity** | Transforms information through sustained integration |

Exposure alone does **not** imply learning.

Repeated continuity enables integration.

### Hypothesis H5

> **Continuity is a stronger predictor of mastery than exposure.**

---

## Activity vs Continuity

**Activity may occur without continuity.**

Examples:

- Random practice
- Repeated mistakes
- Unintegrated repetition

| Concept | Characteristic |
|---------|----------------|
| **Activity** | Behaviour in the domain |
| **Continuity** | Cumulative development with progression |

Continuity requires **cumulative development**.

Continuity implies **progression**.

---

## Integration

**Integration is the central mechanism of learning continuity.**

### Definition

> **Integration** is the process through which new information becomes connected to existing structures and increasingly available for future use.

### Potential integration mechanisms

- Reflection
- Simulation
- Explanation
- Comparison
- Teaching
- Application
- Experimentation
- Discussion
- Consolidation

### Hypothesis H2

> **Continuity increases integration.**

---

## Continuity and Observation

**Learning changes future observation.**

As continuity develops, observation increasingly activates:

```
Attention
  ↓
Memory
  ↓
Knowledge
```

without deliberate retrieval.

The learner begins to observe **patterns previously invisible**.

### Hypothesis H1

> **Learning continuity increases observation capacity.**

---

## Continuity and Expertise

**Expertise is not merely accumulated knowledge.**

**Expertise emerges through integrated continuity.**

As continuity increases:

```
Observation
  ↓
Knowledge
```

becomes progressively **compressed**.

The distance between observation and useful action **decreases**.

### Hypotheses H3 and expertise

| ID | Statement |
|----|-----------|
| **H3** | Integration reduces the distance between observation and knowledge |
| **Expertise** | Expertise is continuity expressed as capability |

---

## Continuity and Recall

**Learning continuity improves retrieval accessibility.**

Knowledge becomes:

- Easier to access
- Faster to access
- More reliable to access
- More transferable

### Hypothesis (recall)

> **Integrated knowledge is more accessible than isolated knowledge.**

---

## Continuity and Simulation

**Simulation accelerates continuity.**

Examples:

- Candidate moves
- Imagined futures
- Mental rehearsal
- Thought experiments

Simulation allows learning to continue **even without direct reality exposure**.

### Federation observation

| Domain | Simulation mode |
|--------|-----------------|
| **ChessGuide** | Internal simulation |
| **Finkairos** | External simulation |

Both contribute to **continuity**.

### Hypothesis H4

> **Simulation accelerates continuity.**

---

## Continuity and Engagement

**Engagement influences continuity.**

### Potential signals

- Curiosity
- Enjoyment
- Intrinsic motivation
- Exploration
- Voluntary return

### Hypothesis H6

> **Positive engagement increases continuity.**

```
Positive engagement → increased continuity → increased integration → increased transformation
```

---

## Continuity and Biology

**Learning continuity is influenced by biological continuity.**

### Potential contributors

- Sleep
- Recovery
- Energy
- Stress
- Rhythms
- Health trajectories

### Future federation relationship

| **BioChronos** | Biological continuity |
| **ChessGuide** | Learning continuity |

### Hypothesis H8

> **Biological continuity influences learning continuity.**

---

## Continuity and Mentorship

**Mentors accelerate continuity.**

Examples:

- Socratic dialogue
- Coaching
- Guided discovery
- Demonstration
- Explanation

Mentorship **increases the rate of integration**.

### Future federation relationship

**Laris** serves as **pedagogical continuity support**.

### Hypothesis H7

> **Mentorship accelerates continuity.**

---

## Continuity and Transformation

**Transformation is the outcome of learning continuity.**

Evidence may include:

- Improved observation
- Improved judgment
- Improved transfer
- Improved stewardship
- Improved performance
- Improved adaptability

### Hypothesis H9

> **Transformation is continuity made visible.**

---

## Learning Continuity Interpretation

```
Reality
  ↓
Observation
  ↓
Attention
  ↓
Understanding
  ↓
Knowledge
  ↓
Wisdom
  ↓
Stewardship
  ↓
Transformation
```

The chain represents a **continuity process** rather than a sequence of isolated events.

**Longitudinal learning** studies how progression occurs across the chain **over time**.

Consistent with [CB-000A — Longitudinal Learning Model](../chessbuddy/CB-000A-longitudinal-learning-model.md) and [CG-FLL-002](CG-FLL-002-learning-semantics.md).
